# Threat Model (PROPOSED design; not an audit)

> This is a design-time threat model. No security audit has been performed or is claimed.
> Likelihood: L/M/H under the assumption of a small federated validator set and an internet-facing
> status endpoint. Impact: L/M/H/Critical for relying enterprises.

Trust boundaries TB-1…TB-7 are defined in [SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md §2](SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md#2-trust-boundaries-relevant-to-conformance).

| # | Threat | Attacker objective | TB | Existing controls (code) | Missing controls | L | Impact | Proposed mitigations | Detection | Tests | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T01 | Compromised validator | Forge passes or violations | TB-2/3 | Pinned-key verification pattern (`executor-receipt/verifier.ts`) | Validator registry, quarantine for conformance | M | H | k-of-distinct-operator corroboration; key rotation; quarantine on contradiction (X `SourceTrustRegistry` pattern) | Cross-validator disagreement rate | Corroboration unit tests; G10 scenarios | One validator can delay (investigation) but not decide |
| T02 | Malicious validator (competitor) | Get a rival suspended | TB-3 | — | Asymmetric single-report rule | M | H | SR-3: single report → investigation only; suspension needs k operators or investigation timeout | Report-without-corroboration counter per validator | "single malicious validator cannot suspend" test | Investigation timeout (SR-4) can be abused by causing validator unavailability → monitored |
| T03 | Validator collusion | False pass or false fail | TB-2 | — | Operator independence policy | L–M | Critical | Distinct legal operators; random assignment; ≥3 operators for disputes; human review of reinstatements | Statistical outlier detection | Assignment randomness tests | Collusion of k operators defeats the system — governance/contract control |
| T04 | Forged evidence | Fake observations | TB-3 | Ed25519 + domain separation (`authority-authenticity`) | Observation domain | M | H | Signature over all fields; challenge nonce echo | Signature-failure metrics | D3 negative tests (each rejection code) | Key theft (T12) |
| T05 | Replayed evidence | Reuse an old pass | TB-3 | Nonce guards (P `FederatedAuthorityReplayGuard`), idempotency patterns | Conformance replay store | M | H | `eventId` uniqueness; `(challengeId, validatorId)` uniqueness; nonce echo; freshness | `REPLAYED` counter | Replay tests | Low |
| T06 | Platform impersonation | Obtain credential for another platform | TB-1 | Tenant scoping (Assurance store) | Platform ownership proof | M | H | Signed testing authorization from platform's registered key; organization binding | Registration audit | Subject-binding tests | Depends on onboarding KYC ([CC-D-15](DECISION_REGISTER.md#cc-d-15)) |
| T07 | False conformance (fixture ≠ production) | Pass on a clean fixture, run a bypassing production build | TB-1 | — | Deployment attestation | **H** | Critical | G08 digest checks against an attested release manifest; scope label "synthetic/staging" in assertions; production testing only when authorized | Digest mismatch | G08 tests | **Significant** until production endpoints are authorized; must be disclosed in every assertion |
| T08 | Unauthorized suspension (insider) | Suspend a customer | TB-5 | AUTH-03 dual-human pattern | Conformance authority format | L | H | Automatic suspensions only via engine rules; manual suspension requires 2 humans; all transitions signed and chained | Transition audit | Lifecycle tests | Low |
| T09 | Stale ACTIVE state | Keep using a suspended credential | TB-6 | FV `validUntil` + high-water mark | Status registry | M | H | ≤15 min assertions; online nonce mode; `freshUntil` check | Verifier telemetry (optional) | STATUS_VERIFICATION §2 tests | ≤15 min for offline verifiers |

FV-P4D) |
| T11 | Signing-key compromise (status issuer) | Forge statuses | TB-4 | External signer reference (`src/enterprise/external-authority-signer`) | HSM / custody decision | L | Critical | Separate custody, key rotation with overlapping validity, published key set, revocation of key | Anchored key-set digests | Key rotation tests | Window until rotation |
| T12 | Validator key compromise | Forge observations | TB-2 | Pinned keys | Revocation list | M | H | Key revocation propagated to D3 before next window; corroboration limits damage | Anomalies | Revoked-key tests | Low with k ≥ 2 |
| T13 | XRPL anchoring failure | Gap in history | TB-7 | `resolveValidation`, idempotent resubmit | Anchor adapter | M | L | Non-blocking anchors; back-fill; batch roots | `indeterminate` age | Anchor retry tests | Low |
| T14 | Blockchain outage | Block suspensions | TB-7 | — | — | L | L (by design) | Suspension never depends on chain | — | "suspension with chain down" test | None |
| T15 | Selective telemetry omission | Hide violations | TB-1 | — | — | H | M | Validators test actively; SDK telemetry never sufficient | Missing-evidence (G09) | G09 tests | Low |

| T17 | Unauthorized SDK downgrade | Run vulnerable SDK | TB-1 | Lockfile checks (`check-compatibility-lock.mjs`) | Runtime SDK attestation | M | H | CR-12; SDK version observed by validators | Version mismatch | G08/CR-12 tests | Depends on observability of version |
| T18 | Malicious profile update | Weaken requirements | TB-4 | Golden-digest discipline (rn.sv) | Profile registry | L | H | Content-addressed profiles; two-person approval; credentials bound to profile version | Digest change | Profile digest test | Low |
| T19 | Cross-tenant contamination | Evidence from org A used for org B | TB-3 | Assurance `requireAccessToAssuranceOrganization`, `EVIDENCE_WRONG_TENANT` | Conformance store scoping | M | H | Organization id in every key; store-level checks | Binding failures | Tenant tests | Low |
| T20 | Privacy leakage | Leak document content or identities | TB-6/7 | FV forbids locators; X publication-consent, SENSITIVE floor | Redaction pipeline | M | H | Digests only; no content in evidence packages; minimal public fields | Redaction lint | Redaction tests | Metadata (that a platform is credentialed) is public by design |
| T21 | Insider override | Reactivate a failing platform | TB-5 | Dual-human authorizer | Lifecycle prohibition | L | Critical | Lifecycle forbids ACTIVE against standing `VERIFIED_FAILURE`; only fresh evidence reinstates | Transition audit | Prohibited-transition tests | Collusion of two insiders + validators |
| T22 | Disaster recovery / rollback | Restore old ACTIVE state | TB-4 | High-water marks (FV), CORE-07 freshness domains | DR runbook | M | H | Monotonic `stateVersion` with external witness; verifiers' high-water marks reject regressions; DR restores require re-evaluation before serving ACTIVE | Rollback detection at verifiers | Restore-from-backup test | Verifiers without history accept a rolled-back but validly signed state until `validUntil` |
