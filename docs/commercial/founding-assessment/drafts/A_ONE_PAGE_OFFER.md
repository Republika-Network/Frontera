## Know who can act on your behalf — and see the evidence behind it

Organizations increasingly let systems and agents act on sensitive assets.
The **Republika Sovereignty Founding Assessment** is a structured,
evidence-based assessment conducted by Republika reviewers. It tells you
whether, on the evidence you supply, your authority, consent, policy,
traceability, isolation and remediation controls hold up, over a scope you
define.

### What you get

- A **human-reviewed assessment** of ten sovereignty controls (SV-01 to
  SV-10), against a frozen, versioned framework identified by digest.
- **Per-control results** — PASS, PARTIAL, FAIL or UNKNOWN — each grounded in
  evidence you supplied. Gaps are named as gaps, not failures.
- A **0–100 posture score** with its limitations stated plainly.
- A **remediation roadmap** derived from the findings.
- A **verifiable delivery package**: you can check that it has not changed,
  and confirm with us, over a separate channel, that we issued it. It is not
  digitally signed.
- **Separate release approval**: a distinct authorized Republika approver,
  who did not perform any of the control reviews, approves your report before
  it is released.

### How it works

1. **Scope** — we agree what is in and out.
2. **Authorize** — you confirm you may share the evidence.
3. **Evidence** — you supply documents, configurations and samples. We never
   access your systems.
4. **Review** — a Republika reviewer concludes each control.
5. **Approve and deliver** — a separate Republika approver approves the
   release, and you receive the package.

Indicative elapsed time: *to be validated* (see the service specification §13).

### Who performs it

Republika wrote the framework, operates the assessment software, and performs
and approves the assessment with its own authorized reviewers. The assessment
is independent of your organization, not of the framework or the tooling. No
external auditor reviews it.

### What it is not

Not a certification, audit opinion, legal or regulatory compliance opinion,
guarantee against cyberattacks, or blanket assurance that autonomous agents
are safe. It is point-in-time, sample-based, and relies on the evidence you
provide: a PASS records a reviewer's conclusion from that evidence, not a
guarantee that the evidence is authentic or complete.
